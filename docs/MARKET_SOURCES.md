# Market sources (external figures, cited, no model)

Context figures for the introduction and the impact discussion. These are external, published figures quoted exactly
from their sources. None of them is a measurement from this repo, none goes into docs/NUMBERS_REGISTER.md, and no
number below feeds any computation here. All pages were fetched on 2026-10-22.

How quotes are marked: a quote in plain quotation marks is a complete sentence or bullet as it appears on the page.
A quote ending in "[...]" is an exact fragment: the page's sentence continues past it, and only the quoted part is
claimed. Re-open the page before quoting the rest.

## 1. AI PC shipments (IDC)

No current (2026) IDC or Canalys AI PC share figure could be confirmed from a primary page (see "Left out" below).
The only confirmed IDC AI PC figures are a 2024 forecast. Use them only as a dated forecast.

- **IDC, "An Investor's Guide to AI Everywhere"** (IDC blog), published 2024-05-31, fetched 2026-10-22.
  https://www.idc.com/resource-center/blog/an-investors-guide-to-ai-everywhere/
  - "By 2028, we expect this proportion to reach almost 60% of the market at 166.4 million AI PC units."
  - "[...] forecasts 54.2 million AI PC units shipped in 2024 representing 21% of the total PC market." (end of a
    longer sentence)
- Caveat from the same publisher: **IDC, "Higher ASPs, lower unit volumes: How the memory crisis is reshaping the PC
  and smartphone outlook"**, published 2026-02-26, fetched 2026-10-22.
  https://www.idc.com/resource-center/blog/higher-asps-lower-unit-volumes-how-the-memory-crisis-is-reshaping-the-pc-and-smartphone-outlook/
  - "The memory mix-down trend is particularly concerning for the AI PC category."
  - This page gives no AI PC unit numbers. Read together, the 2024 forecast above should be treated as likely
    out of date.

## 3. Agent adoption in software teams (Stack Overflow)

- **Stack Overflow, "The results of the 2026 Developer Survey are here!"** (Ryan Donovan, Stack Overflow Blog),
  published 2026-10-06, fetched 2026-10-22. "more than 30,000 people took the survey"; the chart behind the agent
  figures is captioned "Based on 16,664 respondents from the Developer Survey 2026."
  https://stackoverflow.blog/2026/10/06/the-results-of-the-2026-developer-survey-are-here/
  - "Many of those AI users live in coding agents (66%) and chatbots (63%)."
  - "The agents of choice are Claude Code (66%) and GitHub Copilot (59%)."
  - "73% of respondents who indicate using AI coding assistants/agents use them daily," [...]
  - The 66% shares are of AI users, not of all respondents.
- **Stack Overflow, "Agents on a leash: Agentic AI remains mostly single-agent and monitored at work"** (Erin Yepis,
  Stack Overflow Blog), published 2026-05-27, fetched 2026-10-22. A separate pulse survey: "1,100 developers and
  working professionals responded to our survey from late April" [...]. Do not mix with the annual survey.
  https://stackoverflow.blog/2026/05/27/agents-on-a-leash-agentic-ai-remains-mostly-monitored-at-work/
  - "This new survey shows 59% use agents at work at any frequency, compared to 31% in the 2025 Developer Survey."
