## 3. Law
L_hist = (B_BR - a*T)/k - T for unchunked turns; with a 256 cap the a*T term becomes a*256. The
scheduler grows the KV pool to cover the whole turn before prefilling it, so the footprint at the
failing turn is k*(L_hist + T) + a*step.
